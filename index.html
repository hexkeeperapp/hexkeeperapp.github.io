Made for the messy reality

Most sole traders don't run a separate business account — they run business through a personal one. The big accounting apps assume the opposite, so importing a statement means wading through hundreds of personal transactions.

Hexkeeper flips that. What you deliberately record is the source of truth; bank import is just an optional catch-up inbox. Only the transactions you mark as business ever reach your BAS — so personal spending can't leak into the figures you lodge. It's correct by design.
